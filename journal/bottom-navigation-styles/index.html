8 bottom navs
worth stealing.

A small part of the screen. A big part of how your app feels.

Try every style, find the one that fits, and copy the complete React component right beside it.

Same destinations. Different character.

Bottom navigation puts your app’s main destinations within reach. The structure should stay predictable; the shape, surface, and active state can give it personality. These eight approaches overlap: a floating bar can use glass, a pill highlight, or an expanding label.

Every example below works. Switch destinations to try the active states. Use Tab to reach a control, arrow keys to move between destinations, or Home and End to jump.
